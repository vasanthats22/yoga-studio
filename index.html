Our Space

A studio built around breath — not performance.

We opened our doors in 2015 with a simple idea: yoga should feel like coming home, not competing. No mirrors, no rankings, no advanced-pose worship. Just you, your breath, and a room full of people doing the same honest work.

Every class is capped at twelve mats, so your teacher can actually see you, correct you gently, and offer modifications that fit your body — not the person next to you.

  • Certified RYT-200+ teachers only
  • Mats, blocks, straps & blankets provided
  • Beginner-friendly — no flexibility required
  • Herbal tea after every evening class
