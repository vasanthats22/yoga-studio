Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, we recommend booking online at least a day ahead — classes are capped at 12 mats and often fill up.

Is there parking nearby?

Street parking is free after 6 pm, and there's a paid lot one block east of the studio on Serenity Lane.

Anything comfortable that lets you move freely. You'll be barefoot — socks and shoes come off at the door.
