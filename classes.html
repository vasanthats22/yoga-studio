What to Expect

Your first visit

Everything you need is already here.

Come as you are. Mats, props, tea — all taken care of.

The room

12 mats. No mirrors. No rush.

Every class is capped at twelve students so your teacher can actually see you — correct you gently, and offer modifications that fit your body. The space is warm, quiet, and filled with natural light.

Arrive 10 min early Sign in, set up your mat, and breathe before class begins.
Barefoot on the mat Socks off, phones away, shoulders down.
Herbal tea after class A cup of tea and a few quiet minutes after every evening session — no need to rush home.
